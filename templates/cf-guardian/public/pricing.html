Frequently Asked Questions

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?
GEO is the practice of optimizing your website for AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Unlike traditional SEO which targets Google, GEO ensures your content is discoverable and citable by AI models. We check 16 GEO signals including llms.txt, structured data completeness, content depth, and AI crawler accessibility.
How does the free trial work?

Scans run daily at 9:00 AM UTC for all active sites. You can also trigger manual scans on demand from the dashboard.
